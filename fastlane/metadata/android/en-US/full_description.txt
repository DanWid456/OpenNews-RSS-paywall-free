OpenNews collects news from RSS feeds and shows you only articles you can read without a subscription. The app detects articles behind a paywall and hides them before you tap on them.

HOW IT WORKS
• Region and language are detected automatically on first launch – no location permission needed.
• You choose your topics: headlines, national, world, politics, business, technology & science, sport, culture, health, and life & everyday.
• Every article is checked in two stages: first by known signs in the feed, then by the article page itself. The result is saved, so each article is checked only once.
• At the top of your feed you can see how many paywalled articles were hidden.

FEATURES
• Three views: compact, with thumbnail, or as large cards
• Day view: go back day by day
• Save, share and "More on this topic"
• Read articles marked with a green check
• Duplicate stories from several feeds are merged
• Hide video posts if you like
• Mute words, turn individual sources on or off
• Paywall rate per source
• Home screen widget with the latest headlines, freely resizable
• Light and dark mode
• Interface in German, French, Italian, English and Spanish
• Older articles are deleted automatically, saved ones are kept

YOUR OWN FEEDS
Add up to four RSS or Atom feeds of your own for free – by address, keyword or OPML import. With Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription) there is no limit. If you subscribe to a source, you can turn the filter off for it.

PRIVACY
OpenNews needs no account and has no server of its own: everything runs on your device. The app collects no personal data and contains no ads or tracking.

PLEASE NOTE
OpenNews does not bypass paywalls. Articles open on the publisher's website. Detection is automatic and can occasionally be wrong – so you can also hide individual articles by hand.
